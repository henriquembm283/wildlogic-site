Spirekeeper privacy policy

Spirekeeper does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your progress is saved only on your own device.Last updated 11 November 2026. Applies to Spirekeeper on Android, Steam and the web.

Who we are

Spirekeeper is made and published by WILDLOGIC - Sistemas de Informática, Unipessoal, Lda (NIPC 519107276), Caldas da Rainha, Portugal. You can reach us at wildlogiclda@gmail.com.

What the game saves

The game keeps your progress and settings on your device, so you can carry on where you stopped: your levels, stars, unlocks, heroes and options like volume and quality. It never leaves your device unless you choose to copy a save code yourself.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names or email addresses.
  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reports.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Internet access on Android

The Android app has the standard internet permission. The game does not use it to send any data about you. It only matters if you tap a link that opens outside the game, such as this website.

Stores

When you download or buy Spirekeeper from Google Play, Steam or itch.io, that store handles your account and payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Your save lives on your device, so you control it. On Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Settings. In a browser, clear the site data for the game. On Steam, delete the game's local files.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us any question about your data at wildlogiclda@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling personal data, we will update this page first and change the date.
